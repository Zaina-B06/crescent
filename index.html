Meet the dentist

Expertise grounded in global education and local compassion.

Dr. Nidhi Handa

Dr. Nidhi Handa received her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) from Boston University Dental School in 2011. Since then, she has established herself among the top dentists in Wheeling, IL, with patients traveling far to seek her expertise in preventive, restorative, and cosmetic dentistry.

She loves caring for patients of all ages, heavily emphasizing patient education to maintain comprehensive dental health. Dr. Handa stays at the forefront of the latest clinical advancements through rigorous continuing education. Outside the clinic, she enjoys spending time with her family and friends.

Dr. Hidayathulla Khan

Dr. Hidayathulla Khan received his Doctor of Dental Medicine from the University of Illinois and holds a Master's degree in Dental Surgery from Rajiv Gandhi University. He is recognized as a premier provider in Wheeling, IL for his expert management of preventative, restorative, and complex cosmetic dentistry.

Dedicated to providing exceptional care for all ages, Dr. Khan strongly believes in empowering his patients through education. He frequently attends advanced continuing education programs to master the latest dental technologies and treatments. Outside of practice, he enjoys spending quality time with friends and family.
